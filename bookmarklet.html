kooba-helper bookmarklet

Drag the button below to your bookmarks bar (or right-click → "Bookmark this link"). When you are on an Abook listing, click it to add search links for NZBIndex, BinSearch, NZBKing, a “Copy Title” button and an NZBDonkey highlight block.

Works in Chromium, Brave and Firefox. Unlike the userscript, it does not run automatically — click it on every page you want to use it on.
